# Transform Collective

Build a premium, dark-themed demo website for a British luxury fashion brand that designs transformative garments — clothing that physically converts from one silhouette to another (e.g. a shirt that transforms into a dress). This is a high-end ecommerce concept site: sophisticated "quiet luxury" with warmth, not sterile or synthetic-looking. Products are the commercial focus of this site — the story exists to support the shopping experience, not replace it.

CRITICAL — AVOID "AI-GENERATED" LOOK

- Do not use glossy, overly-symmetrical, airbrushed, or uncanny-looking imagery/video for the hero or products. Avoid the typical AI-render aesthetic: hyper-smooth skin, impossible lighting, generic stock-model poses, exaggerated bokeh, or overly perfect studio composites

- Favour naturalistic, editorial photography direction: slightly imperfect framing, real fabric movement, candid mid-motion moments, natural or warm tungsten lighting, visible texture (fabric weave, stitching, skin texture) — like a real fashion shoot, not a synthetic render

- Keep hero video/imagery grounded and believable — a real person genuinely adjusting/transforming the garment, not a dreamy abstract loop

AESTHETIC DIRECTION

- Dark theme: deep charcoal/near-black backgrounds (#0A0A0A – #121212), not pure black

- Accent: one warm muted tone (champagne gold, warm bone, terracotta-taupe) used sparingly

- Elegant serif headings, clean sans-serif body, comfortable line-lengths, conversational rather than corporate copy

- Subtle warmth: soft film-grain, gentle rounded card corners — but restrained, not decorative for its own sake

HERO SECTION

- Autoplaying looped background video (muted, no controls), grounded/naturalistic per the direction above, showing the garment transformation

- Minimal overlay text + single CTA that leads straight to Shop, not Story

- Keep it short — this is a doorway into shopping, not the main event

PAGE PRIORITY (products first)

1. Home — hero video → featured products grid (prominent, above the fold after hero) → "Transform" showcase module → short one-line story teaser (a sentence, not a section) with a link to Our Story → email signup. The story teaser should be visually minor compared to the product grid

2. Shop / Collection — full product grid, filterable (Shirts, Dresses, Convertible), large clear product imagery as the dominant visual element on the page, hover-reveal second angle, price clearly visible

3. Individual product page template — this is the most important template on the site: large gallery with zoom, multiple angles, on-body shots, transformation video clip, size/fit guide, care instructions, prominent "Add to Bag", trust details

4. How It Transforms — dedicated page for the step-by-step transformation explainer

5. Our Story — moved deeper in the nav, kept concise: founder note, craftsmanship, sustainability angle — supporting content, not the site's centrepiece

6. Journal / Lookbook — editorial page, styling stories, seasonal lookbooks

7. Contact / Stockists — simple form, social links

8. Footer — Instagram strip, newsletter signup, legal placeholders

UX REQUIREMENTS

- Fully responsive, mobile-first

- Sticky navbar: logo, then Shop first, then How It Transforms / Our Story / Journal / Contact — order reflects priority

- Cart drawer (slide-in)

- Product grid should never be more than one click from the homepage hero

TONE FOR PLACEHOLDER COPY

Confident, warm, editorial — but keep product-page copy practical and sales-focused (materials, fit, transformation instructions), saving the more narrative voice for Our Story and Journal only.

Generate 6-8 sample products (name, price in GBP, one-line description, materials line) themed around convertible/transformable garments, with product imagery direction per the naturalistic guidance above, and 2-3 sample Journal/Lookbook entries.
